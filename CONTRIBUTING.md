# Contributing a Hack Day submission

Thank you for taking part in Hack Day!

Please follow the submission steps in the [main README](README.md). Your Pull Request should add one folder at:

```text
submissions/your-team-name/
```

Keep all project code, assets, documentation, and setup instructions inside that folder. Do not change another team's submission or commit secrets and personal data.

By opening a Pull Request, you confirm that your team has permission to share the submitted content.
